How to Add to Your Signature

  1. Copy the HTML — Click "Copy HTML" on your favorite badge above.
  2. Open your email settings:
    • Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → paste
    • Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose → Email signature → paste
    • Apple Mail: Mail → Preferences → Signatures → paste
  3. Save — That's it! Every email now helps spread the word.
