How it works

1 · Pick

Choose or write one prediction per month. We turn your list into a small text file on your device.

2 · Seal

A unique hash of that file is anchored to the blockchain. It can't be changed or backdated, not even by us.

3 · Reveal

Each month, check your pick and share it. Anyone can verify your file against the timestamp, anytime.

Questions

Can anyone see my prediction?
No. Only the hash is recorded, and it can't be turned back into your words. You decide if and when to reveal it.

Why not just post it online?
Posts can be edited or deleted, and screenshots can be faked. A blockchain timestamp is independent, permanent and checkable by anyone.

What do I get?
Your prediction file, plus a certificate and a public verification link once the timestamp is anchored, usually within about an hour.

Is it $2.89 per prediction?
No. All 12 months go into one sealed file, so it's $2.89 for the whole year.

Can I do this with friends?
That's the idea. Share the page, everyone seals their own list, and you compare each month.
